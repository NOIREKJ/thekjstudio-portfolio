## About the project

NOIRE is a personal assistant app designed to help you manage what truly matters — your assets, your time, your routines — in one quiet, considered space.

Built with a minimal dark interface, NOIRE focuses on clarity over clutter. It's designed for people who want their tools to disappear so they can focus on the things that don't.

## Key features

- **Unified asset management** — Bring everything you care about into one place
- **Minimal, dark-first interface** — Designed to feel calm, not demanding
- **Built for focus** — Less noise, more signal
